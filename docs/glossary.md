# Glossary

The vocabulary of Stratus, with the meaning the code gives each term and where
that meaning is defined. One alphabetical list; each entry ends with the file
and the function or field that makes it true. Dates and counts are seed values
that `npm run data` reproduces.

The story behind the words is the root [README](../README.md). The mechanics
are in [architecture.md](architecture.md), [data-model.md](data-model.md),
[rules.md](rules.md), [permissions.md](permissions.md) (rendered whole from
`src/lib/auth.js`) and [seed-and-arcs.md](seed-and-arcs.md); the commands are
in [runbook.md](runbook.md); the metrics as SQL are in
[analytics.md](analytics.md); the layer specifications are under
[specs/](specs/README.md).

## Terms

- **acknowledge / acked** — `acked` is a boolean on a sortie and on a coverage row; crew acknowledge their own seat, and the seed leaves only the planned day's rows unacknowledged. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`acknowledge`, which authorizes `sortie.ack` and returns a copy with `acked: true`) and `src/lib/auth.js` (`ownSeat`).
- **actor** — The persona performing a mutation. Every transition in `src/lib/rules.js` takes the actor as its first argument and calls `authorize()` before it touches anything (R9); the actor is stamped as `by` on a timeline event, `assigned_by` on a sortie and on a coverage row, `granted_by` on a rating or qualification, and `by` on an audit row. Defined in `src/lib/auth.js` (`can`, `authorize`).
- **airframe** — One of the three Windz types: Levant, Harmattan, Sirocco. Each carries a role string and the windows it flies (Levant AM, PM and NIGHT; Harmattan and Sirocco AM and PM); a tail belongs to one, a request asks for one, a rating is per airframe (R1) and a tail of the wrong airframe is an R3 block. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`AIRFRAMES`), exported as `meta.airframes`.
- **analytics notebook** — [analytics.md](analytics.md): the dispatch metrics as SQL queries in `sql/analytics/`, run against `data/stratus.sqlite` and rendered with their results by `scripts/render_analytics.py` (`npm run analytics`). The app never reads it. Defined in `scripts/render_analytics.py`.
- **arc** — A pattern planted in the seed so a view has something to say: arcs 1–3 in the RCCA half (the sim-only crosswind failure, the telemetry gap, the regression) and D1–D4 in the dispatch half (the misattributed shortage, the bring-up surge, the grounding cascade, the desk shortage). Defined in the module docstring of `generate_data.py`; the detail is in [seed-and-arcs.md](seed-and-arcs.md).
- **artifacts** — `run.artifacts` is `{ logs, telemetry, video }`. `video` is `null` for Simulation (not applicable) and `false` when missing; `missingArtifacts()` reports `logs` and `telemetry` when falsy and `video` only when `=== false`. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`default_artifacts`) and `src/lib/helpers.js` (`missingArtifacts`).
- **asset** — A tail as the data knows it: `asset_id`, `airframe`, `config` (`baseline` or `instrumented`), `windows`, the snapshot fields `status`, `reserved_by`, `status_note` and `status_since` as of today, and `status_history`. `makeDb()` indexes assets as `db.asset`. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`ASSET_ROWS`, `STATUS_EVENTS`); see *tail*.
- **asset status** — `available`, `grounded`, `maintenance` or `reserved`, on a given day. `assetStatusOn(asset, date)` returns the first `status_history` entry covering the date, else `available`; grounded and maintenance tails are unassignable (R3) and leave the utilization denominator, and a reserved tail is assignable only to `reserved_by`. The flat `status` fields are the snapshot as of today. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`assetStatusOn`).
- **asset-window** — One tail in one window on one operating day: the unit of supply and the denominator of asset utilization. `dailyCapacity()` counts `windows.length` for every tail not grounded or in maintenance that day. Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`dailyCapacity`, `demandVsSupply`).
- **assignment / sortie** — The same record: "sortie" on the screen, `assignment` in the data. `assignment_id`, `date`, `window`, `request_id`, `asset_id`, `operator_id`, `pilot_id`, `status` (`planned`, `done` or `scrubbed` in the seed; the schema also admits `in_progress`), `scrub_reason`, `run_id`, `notes`, `assigned_by`, `acked`. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`newAssignment`, the board's constructor) and `generate_data.py` (`new_assignment`, the planner's).
- **audit** — Session-only state in `App.jsx`: actions outside a request's timeline, each `{ at, by, action, target, detail }`, appended by `record()` for grants and revokes, program and desk-settings edits, desk covers and releases, acknowledgements and triage changes. `actionsByRole()` counts the grants under `granted` and `program.priority`, `program.targets` and `desk.settings` under `set`. Defined in `src/App.jsx` (`record`).
- **AV** — The availability warning: a person not rostered for the window yields `{ rule: 'AV', severity: 'warn' }` from `checkAssignment()` and `checkCover()`. A warning, not a block: the coordinator may still place someone who is off shift. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`checkAssignment`, `checkCover`).
- **availability** — `person.availability`: ISO date to the list of windows the person is rostered. `availableOn(person, date, window)` is the question. The generator derives it from a shift pattern (`day` is AM and PM on weekdays, `night` is NIGHT on weekdays, `swing` is PM and NIGHT on weekdays, `day+wkend` adds the surge weekend) minus vacations and a 5% odd day off. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`availableOn`) and `generate_data.py` (`pattern_windows`).
- **board** — The Dispatch view: one day's plan, with the date picker, the desk row, one group per program with a tail by window grid, the rail and the footer. Only dates after today are editable; edits go through the rules and are stamped in the evening of today. Defined in `src/views/DispatchView.jsx`.
- **build / build stage** — `request.build` and `run.build` are version strings (`v2.13.2` to `v2.16.1` in the seed) stamped from a date by `build_for()` over `BUILD_WINDOWS`; the intake form offers the distinct builds seen in runs. `build_stage` is `engineering` or `release_candidate`. The planner defers a request `build_not_ready` while its build's window has not started. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`BUILD_WINDOWS`, `build_for`, `BUILD_START`) and `src/lib/rules.js` (`BUILD_STAGES`).
- **cert push (PLC)** — Arc D1's pressure: May 4–8, when PLC asks for four sorties a day in AM and PM and HM-04 is reserved for it. The two cross-rating flights are deferred twice during it because training is P2. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`PLC_PUSH`, `STATUS_EVENTS`, `daily_demand`).
- **check_rules() and the sanity report** — The generator's two gates. `check_rules()` re-verifies the seed against R1–R4, R8, the desk (R2 extended, R10 as I5) and the actors (I1, I2, I4) before any file is written; the sanity report at the end prints the counts and asserts the load-bearing arcs: churn peaks on the grounding day, each cross-rating flight flies before its rating takes effect, the ratio refuses a showcase on the first day of the run, and tomorrow holds exactly two unscheduled P0. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`check_rules`, the sanity report section).
- **coordinator on duty** — In the seed, the coordinator persona an event defaults to: U-03 (AM) when the stamp is before 14:00, U-04 (PM) after. A request whose requester has no persona is filed by the coordinator on duty. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`coordinator_on`, `requester_user`).
- **coverage / coverer** — One coverage row per (date, window, person): `{ date, window, person_id, assigned_by, acked }`, plus `at` when added on the board. A coverer is the row's person when qualified on that date: `deskCoverers(db, date, window)`. The coordinator rosters coverage (`desk.cover`); `checkCover()` guards going on and `checkUncover()` coming off. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`deskCoverers`, `coverWindow`, `uncoverWindow`); the seed roster by `desk_roster()` in `generate_data.py`.
- **crew requirement** — `request.crew`: `none`, `pilot_only`, `operator_pilot` or `lone_operator` (`CREW_KINDS`, labelled by `CREW_LABELS`). R4 checks the seats match: `operator_pilot` needs an operator and a safety pilot who are different people, `pilot_only` has no operator seat, `lone_operator` needs an operator with `self_pilot` and no pilot seat, `none` takes no crew. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`CREW_KINDS`, `checkAssignment`).
- **cross-rating flight** — The two CQ requests titled "Type rating — Harmattan (name)" for P-011 and P-012: parked to May 27 on their second deferral and flown before 2026-05-29, the day their Harmattan rating takes effect; the sanity report asserts each flew first. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`cq_cross_ids`, `defer`, `CROSS_RATING_DATE`).
- **currency check** — A CQ request the planner files periodically for a Levant-rated subject, who flies their own check; a subject who is busy elsewhere is a reschedule (`subject_busy`), not a shortage. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`daily_demand`, `crew_for`, `plan_day`).
- **cutoff** — 15:00 local, the intake deadline for the next day's plan: `meta.cutoff_local`, every program's `cutoff_local`, and the default `cutoff` of `isLate()`. The planner builds day D at 15:30 on the day before. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`CUTOFF`) and `src/lib/rules.js` (`isLate`).
- **day (on a timeline event)** — `event.day` is the plan day the event refers to, distinct from `at`, the stamp: a deferral stamped at Tuesday's cutoff is for Wednesday, and `day` is `null` on `submitted`. Deferrals, scrubs, churn and lead time are grouped by `day`; `actionsByRole()` groups by the date of `at`. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`event`) and `sql/schema.sql` (`request_events.day`).
- **deferral / deferral reason** — A request the plan could not include. `deferRequest()` requires a reason from `DEFERRAL_REASONS` (no reason, no deferral: R8), appends a `deferred` event carrying `day`, and sets `status: 'deferred'`, `deferral_reason` and `plan_date` to the day after; the reason `requester_withdrew` withdraws instead. The nine reasons are `no_rated_operator`, `no_rated_pilot`, `no_asset`, `asset_grounded`, `program_over_max`, `build_not_ready`, `late_intake`, `requester_withdrew` and `no_rider_ops`, labelled by `REASON_LABELS` and exported as `meta.deferral_reasons`. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`DEFERRAL_REASONS`, `deferRequest`).
- **demand vs supply** — Per airframe and window: supply is asset-windows on operating days with grounded and maintenance tails excluded; demand is non-withdrawn requests with `needed_by` in range, counted under their first-choice window; `flexible` counts those that asked for more than one window. Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`demandVsSupply`).
- **desk (rider desk)** — Rider operations is a desk, not a seat: qualified rider operators cover windows on comms with riders aboard instead of flying. In the code it is the coverage rows plus `db.desk` (`{ ratio, min_per_window }`, from `meta.rider_desk`), the functions `deskCoverers`, `riderLoad`, `checkCoverage`, `checkCover` and `checkUncover`, and the desk row on the board; R10 is its rule. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (the rider desk section).
- **desk load** — Rider-facing sorties per coverer in a window. `deskByDay()` computes `riders / coverers` per operating day and window (`null` with riders and nobody covering, `0` with neither); `deskLoad()` sums a period, counts the windows at or over the ratio (`atRatio`) and finds the peak day. Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`deskByDay`, `deskLoad`).
- **dispatch layer** — The half of `generate_data.py` below the `dispatch layer` banner: programs, tails, people, personas, the desk roster, requests and the day-by-day planner, all on the `drng` stream. Defined in `generate_data.py` (the banner comment).
- **effective date** — Ratings and qualifications count from a day: `person.ratings_effective[airframe]` and `person.qualifications.rider_ops`. `ratedOn()` and `qualifiedOn()` compare against the day asked about, and a rating with no effective date was always held. A grant on the board needs a date no earlier than `notBefore` (App passes `meta.tomorrow`): history is not rewritten. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`ratedOn`, `qualifiedOn`, `assertGrantDate`).
- **failure / occurrence** — An RCCA item (`F-NNNN`): `title`, `severity`, `triage_status`, `team`, `root_cause` (null until determined), `opened`, `resolved` (stamped only when Verified), `five_whys`, `corrective_action` and `occurrences`, the ids of the runs it groups. A run may be an occurrence of more than one failure. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`add_failure`).
- **grounding** — A `status_history` entry with status `grounded`; its `failure_id` names the RCCA failure the tail is grounded against. `groundingDays()` counts tail-days per failure and the days while the failure was still open; `tailsAgainst()` lists them in triage. Seed: LV-03 and LV-05, Jun 10–18, against arc 3's failure. Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`groundingDays`, `tailsAgainst`) and `generate_data.py` (`STATUS_EVENTS`).
- **heartbeat** — The header strip of the last 60 runs as status-coloured cells. Defined in `src/App.jsx` (`heartbeat`).
- **I1** — Nobody assigns their own request, even a coordinator: `plan.assign` and `plan.reassign` are `not-own` grants, refused as "R9 · you can't assign your own request". In the seed a request a coordinator filed on behalf is placed by the other coordinator, and `check_rules()` asserts `assigned_by` differs from `requester_id`. Defined in `src/lib/auth.js` (`PERMISSIONS`, `ownsRequest`).
- **I2** — Nobody grants their own rating or qualification: `rating.grant`, `rating.revoke` and `qualification.grant` are `not-self` grants. The trainer persona U-15 is also roster person P-001, which is what makes the rule real; `check_rules()` asserts every `granted_by` is a trainer persona whose person differs from the grantee. Defined in `src/lib/auth.js` (`isSelf`).
- **I3** — The shape of the matrix: coordinators consume priority and cannot set it (`program.priority` is authority-only), authority sets priority and cannot assign (`plan.assign` is coordinator-only). Defined in `src/lib/auth.js` (`PERMISSIONS`).
- **I4** — Every actor's role permits the event it stamped. `eventAction()` maps a timeline event to its action; the check runs over every timeline event, every sortie's `assigned_by` (`plan.assign`) and every coverage row's `assigned_by` (`desk.cover`), in `check_rules()` on regeneration and in the seed test. Defined in `src/lib/auth.js` (`eventAction`) and `generate_data.py` (`permits`, `check_rules`).
- **I5** — Every rider-facing sortie flew with qualified coverage at or under ratio: for each (date, window) with rider-facing non-scrubbed sorties, coverers are at least `min_per_window` and sorties at most `ratio × coverers`. Checked in `check_rules()` and in the seed test. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`check_rules`) and `src/lib/rules.test.js`.
- **intake** — Filing a request. `validateRequest(fields, db, { tomorrow })` returns `[{ field, message }]` for every refused field; `newRequest(actor, fields, { id, submittedAt, cutoff, isOpen })` authorizes `request.create`, derives `late` and `plan_date` from the submission time, and stamps the `submitted` event (and, when late, the `late_intake` deferral) by the actor. The form is `IntakeForm` in `src/views/RequestsView.jsx`. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`validateRequest`, `newRequest`).
- **late (R7)** — `request.late` equals `isLate(submitted_at, needed_by, cutoff)`: submitted at or after the cutoff on the day before `needed_by`, or on or after the day itself. A late request plans for the next open day, carries a `late_intake` deferral stamped by the filer (part of `request.create`), and leaves the on-window fulfillment denominator; it is flagged, never absorbed. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`isLate`, `defaultPlanDate`, `lateWarning`).
- **lead time** — Days from the submission date to the `executed` event's `day`, p50 and p90 per program and overall. The percentile is `sorted[min(n - 1, floor(q * n))]`, the same rank rule as `sql/analytics/05_lead_time.sql`. Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`leadTimes`).
- **loop (the loop metric)** — Grounding days attributable to RCCA items: the dispatch cost of the RCCA backlog. `status_history[].failure_id` is the link; a grounded tail on the board opens its failure in Triage, and a failure's grounded tails open the board. Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`groundingDays`) and `sql/schema.sql` (`asset_status_history.failure_id`).
- **misattribution (the tell)** — A deferral logged `no_rated_operator` on a day when a rated operator sat idle (rostered in a requested window, on no sortie there, not on the desk) is refuted by the roster. `misattribution()` returns `{ total, refuted, pilotsShort }`; `idleRated()` treats a coverer as busy, because someone on comms is not idle. Arc D1 plants it: before 2026-04-28 the planner logs PLC pilot shortages as `no_rated_operator`, "per thread consensus". Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`misattribution`, `idleRated`) and `generate_data.py` (`defer`, `MISATTRIBUTED_UNTIL`).
- **needed_by** — `request.needed_by`, the day the requester asked for; intake requires it to be tomorrow or later. The cutoff is measured against the day before it (R7), and on-window fulfillment counts a request once it has passed. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`validateRequest`, `isLate`).
- **north star (on-window fulfillment)** — Of requests not late, not withdrawn and whose `needed_by` is on or before today, the share executed with their latest sortie dated on or before `needed_by` in a requested window, by priority and overall. `riderFulfillment()` splits rider-facing requests from the rest, the number the desk's ratio protects. Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`onWindowFulfillment`, `riderFulfillment`).
- **occurrence-weighted** — Counting events, not items. `paretoRootCauses()` weighs a root cause by `max(1, occurrences.length)` and excludes failures with no root cause; `deferralPareto()` counts every `deferred` event, so a request deferred three times counts three. `failuresByTeam()` is one per failure. Defined in `src/lib/helpers.js` (`paretoRootCauses`) and `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`deferralEvents`).
- **on behalf** — A request filed by a coordinator for a requester who has no persona: `requester_id` is the coordinator on duty, `requester` stays the engineer's name, and the Requests drawer shows the filer's name followed by "· on behalf". I1 then has the other coordinator place it. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`requester_user`) and `src/views/RequestsView.jsx` (`filedBy`).
- **on-window fulfillment** — See *north star*.
- **operating day** — A day on which anyone is rostered: `isOperatingDay(date, db)` is true when some person's `availability[date]` is non-empty. Utilization, demand vs supply and desk load skip other days, and R5 does not flag a standing program under target on them. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`isOperatingDay`).
- **operator** — The crew role `operator` on `person.roles`, the `operator_id` seat on a sortie. R4 requires the role for the seat; a lone operator also needs `self_pilot`. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`checkAssignment`, R4).
- **operator-pilot** — A person holding both the `operator` and `pilot` roles; the seed has three (P-011, P-012, P-014), all `self_pilot`. R4 forbids one person taking both seats of one sortie. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`PERSON_ROWS`).
- **persona** — A `users.json` record `{ user_id, name, title, role, scope, person_id }` the header picker switches between: the title is display, the role is behaviour. State, not identity: `currentUser` in `App.jsx`, and switching it never resets the plan. Crew personas and the trainer carry a `person_id` onto the roster. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`USERS`); the contract is the header comment of `src/lib/auth.js`.
- **pilot (safety pilot)** — The crew role `pilot`, the `pilot_id` seat; the screen says "safety pilot". Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`CREW_LABELS`, R4 in `checkAssignment`) and `src/views/MyDayView.jsx` (`ROLE_WORDS`).
- **pipeline** — Where a run executed: `Simulation`, `HIL Bench` or `Flight`. Only Flight runs link to a sortie through `run.request_id`; video is not applicable to Simulation. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`PIPELINES`).
- **plan churn** — Scrubs plus same-day reassignments per plan day: `churnByDay()` counts `scrubbed` and `reassigned` events by `day`, zero-filled across the range. The sanity report asserts churn peaks on the grounding day. Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`churnByDay`).
- **plan day / plan_date** — `request.plan_date`, the day a request is queued for. Intake sets it to `needed_by`, or the next open day when late; `deferRequest()` moves it to the day after the deferral's `day`; `scheduleRequest()` sets it to the sortie's date; `scrubRequest()` keeps it on the scrubbed sortie's date. A request sits on a day's rail while `plan_date <= date`. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`defaultPlanDate`, `deferRequest`, `scheduleRequest`, `queuedFor`).
- **priority P0–P3** — `PRIORITY_RANK = { P0: 0, P1: 1, P2: 2, P3: 3 }`. A request's `priority` defaults from its program's `priority_default`, which authority sets; the rail and the planner sort P0 first, then submission time, and R6 names a P0 starved by a lower priority. The intake form offers P0–P2, the program dialog P0–P3, and failure `severity` uses the same four values. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`PRIORITY_RANK`).
- **program** — `programs.json`: `program_id`, `code`, `name`, `lead`, `priority_default`, `standing`, `airframes`, `asset_min`, `asset_max`, `cutoff_local`. Authority edits priority and tail targets through `editProgram()`, each behind its own permission and scope; R5 measures a day against `asset_min`–`asset_max`. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`PROGRAMS`) and `src/lib/rules.js` (`editProgram`).
- **program codes** — The six programs that share the fleet: PLC Precision Landing Cert (P0, Harmattan, standing), PN Perception Nightly (P1, Levant, standing), SBU Sirocco Bring-up (P0, Sirocco), URC Urban Route Campaign (P1, Levant), CQ Crew Qualification (P2, Levant and Harmattan, standing) and SF Showcase Flights (P0, Levant). Defined in `generate_data.py` (`PROGRAMS`).
- **qualification (rider_ops)** — The desk qualification, effective-dated like a rating: `person.qualifications.rider_ops` is the day it takes effect; `qualifiedOn(person, date)` is the question. `grantQualification()` adds the `rider_ops` role, the date and `granted_by.rider_ops`; there is no revoke. Seed: 2026-04-01 for the three rider operators, 2026-06-17 for P-009. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`qualifiedOn`, `grantQualification`).
- **queued** — A request still waiting for a sortie: status `submitted` or `deferred` (`isQueued`). The request table orders statuses submitted, deferred, scheduled, executed, withdrawn (`STATUS_ORDER`). Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`isQueued`, `STATUS_ORDER`).
- **R1 · type rating** — Operator and pilot must be rated on the sortie's airframe as of its date; a block. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`checkAssignment`, `ratedOn`).
- **R2 · double booking** — No tail and no person in two sorties in the same date and window; a scrubbed sortie frees its crew, not its slot. Extended to the desk: nobody covers a window twice or flies in a window they cover, and only a coverer can be released. A block. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`checkAssignment`, `checkCover`, `checkUncover`).
- **R3 · asset status** — Grounded and maintenance tails are unassignable, a reserved tail only to the reserving program, and the tail must be the request's airframe and operate the window. A block. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`checkAssignment`, `assetStatusOn`).
- **R4 · crew requirement** — The seats must satisfy `request.crew`; a lone operator needs the operator role and `self_pilot`. A block. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`checkAssignment`).
- **R5 · program tail target** — Distinct non-scrubbed tails per program per day within `asset_min`–`asset_max`: `dayWarnings()` reports `under` (on operating days with demand) and `over`, and `checkAssignment()` warns at edit time when a new tail would pass the cap. A warning. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`dayWarnings`, `programTails`).
- **R6 · P0 starvation** — An unscheduled P0 waits while a lower-priority sortie holds a tail of its airframe in a window it asked for, that day. A warning naming the holder. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`dayWarnings`).
- **R7 · intake cutoff** — See *late*. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`isLate`, `defaultPlanDate`, `lateWarning`).
- **R8 · deferral needs a reason** — A deferral or scrub without a reason from the enum cannot be built: `deferRequest()`, `scrubAssignment()` and `scrubRequest()` throw. Structural. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`validateReason`, `DEFERRAL_REASONS`, `SCRUB_REASONS`).
- **R9 · authorization** — Every mutation carries an actor and is refused when the role lacks the permission, the ownership, the scope or the self-exclusion, with the role and the action named: "R9 · coordinator only (signed in as Observer)". The UI disables the control with the message as its tooltip; the enforcement is `authorize()` inside the transition. Defined in `src/lib/auth.js` (`can`, `authorize`); every message is listed in [permissions.md](permissions.md).
- **R10 · rider desk** — A rider-facing sortie needs at least `min_per_window` qualified coverers in its window and `load + 1 <= ratio × coverers`, else it is blocked ("R10 · desk at 3:1, ratio is 2"); `checkCoverage()` colours the desk row red, amber, green or quiet, and `checkUncover()` holds the rule on release. There is no waiver. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`checkAssignment`, `checkCoverage`, `checkUncover`).
- **rail** — The board's aside of unscheduled requests. `queuedFor(date, db)` returns the requests live for the day (submitted or deferred with `plan_date <= date`) plus those deferred from it, as history, sorted by priority then submission time; the unscheduled count covers only the live ones. Tomorrow's rail holds exactly two P0 in the seed. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`queuedFor`) and `src/views/DispatchView.jsx` (`rail`).
- **rating (type rating)** — `person.ratings`: the airframes a person may crew, each optionally effective-dated in `ratings_effective`. `ratedOn(person, airframe, date)` is the only way to ask, and R1 blocks an unrated seat. The trainer grants and revokes (`grantRating`, `revokeRating`), never their own, and `granted_by[airframe]` records who; in the seed P-011 and P-012 earn Harmattan on 2026-05-29. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`ratedOn`, `grantRating`, `revokeRating`).
- **ratio / min_per_window** — The desk's two settings, `db.desk` from `meta.rider_desk` (`{ ratio: 2, min_per_window: 1 }` in the seed and in `DEFAULT_DESK`): the rider-facing sorties one coverer may hold, and the coverers a window needs before it takes riders. `setDeskSettings()` (the PM or the rider ops lead) bounds both to 1–4 and returns exactly those two keys; a waiver is not a setting. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`DEFAULT_DESK`, `DESK_BOUNDS`, `setDeskSettings`).
- **reassignment (same-day)** — Placing a scrubbed request on another free tail and crew the same day: the `reassigned` timeline event, action `plan.reassign`, counted as churn. The planner does it in `execute_day()` in the same or a later window with R10 re-checked; the matrix carries the permission ahead of a board control. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`execute_day`) and `src/lib/auth.js` (`EVENT_ACTION`).
- **request** — The unit of intake (`RQ-NNNN` in the seed, `RQ-LNNN` from the form): `request_id`, `program_id`, `requester`, `title`, `build`, `build_stage`, `crew`, `windows`, `airframe`, `priority`, `supporting_team`, `submitted_at`, `needed_by`, `plan_date`, `status` (`submitted`, `scheduled`, `executed`, `deferred` or `withdrawn`), `late`, `deferral_reason`, `timeline`, `rider_facing`, `requester_id`. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`newRequest`) and `generate_data.py` (`add_request`).
- **rescheduled** — A timeline event with no reason: the planner re-queues a request without deferring it (a scrub that was not a grounding, a currency check whose subject was busy, the cross-rating flights parked to May 27). `eventAction()` treats it as `plan.defer` and `actionsByRole()` counts it under `deferred`; the board never emits it. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`defer`, `plan_day`, `execute_day`).
- **rider operator** — The crew role `rider_ops`: covers the desk and holds no seat; the screen says "rider operator". Seed: P-018 (day), P-019 (swing), P-020 (night), and P-009, an operator who qualifies for the desk on 2026-06-17. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`RIDER_OPS_ROWS`) and `src/views/MyDayView.jsx` (`ROLE_WORDS`).
- **rider-facing** — `request.rider_facing`: riders aboard, so the desk must cover the window (R10). Set at intake and forced on for a showcase (`validateRequest()` refuses an SF request without riders); in the seed always true for SF, about a fifth of PN and URC, never PLC, SBU or CQ. `riderLoad()` counts the non-scrubbed rider-facing sorties in a window. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`validateRequest`, `riderLoad`) and `generate_data.py` (`add_request`).
- **RNG streams** — The generator's three seeded sources: the module `random` seeded 42 for runs and failures, `drng = random.Random(4242)` for the dispatch layer, `rrng = random.Random(4243)` for the rider-facing flags. Changing the dispatch section never re-rolls runs or failures (only the run-to-sortie link, `run.request_id`, follows the planner); a `drng` draw added or removed before Jun 15 re-rolls arcs D1–D3. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`random.seed`, `drng`, `rrng`).
- **role (app role vs crew role)** — App roles live on personas (`user.role`: `requester`, `coordinator`, `authority`, `trainer`, `crew`, `observer`) and decide what the matrix allows; crew roles live on people (`person.roles`: `operator`, `pilot`, `rider_ops`) and describe seats and the desk. The header badge shows the app role. Defined in `src/lib/auth.js` (`ROLES`, `ROLE_LABELS`) and `sql/schema.sql` (`users.role`, `person_roles`).
- **root-cause Pareto** — Root causes ranked by occurrence count with a cumulative percentage, excluding failures not yet root-caused: the Analytics chart. Six categories in the seed. Defined in `src/lib/helpers.js` (`paretoRootCauses`) and `generate_data.py` (`ROOT_CAUSES`).
- **roster** — The people (`persons.json`, 20 in the seed: 17 crew and three rider operators): `person_id`, `name`, `roles`, `ratings`, `self_pilot`, `ratings_effective`, `availability`, `qualifications`, `granted_by`. "Rostered" means available in that window. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`PERSON_ROWS`, `RIDER_OPS_ROWS`).
- **run** — One test execution (`TR-2026-NNNN`): `date`, `pipeline`, `scenario`, `build`, `status` (`pass`, `fail` or `blocked`), `duration_min`, `owner`, `artifacts`, and `request_id` for a Flight run linked to a flown sortie (else `null`). Runs are never mutated; today is the last run date. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`add_run`).
- **scenario** — The named test case a run exercises, one of twelve fixed strings; `SCENARIO_POOL` says which a program's sorties plausibly produce and links Flight runs to sorties. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`SCENARIOS`, `SCENARIO_POOL`).
- **scope** — An authority persona's reach, `user.scope`: `'all'` (the PM), a list of program ids (a program lead) or `['desk']` (the rider ops lead). `inScope()` and `deskScope()` are the predicates behind the `scope` and `desk` grants; the refusal reads "R9 · PN is outside your scope". Defined in `src/lib/auth.js` (`inScope`, `deskScope`).
- **scrub** — Cancelling a planned sortie on the day. `scrubAssignment()` sets `status: 'scrubbed'` and `scrub_reason` from `SCRUB_REASONS` (the deferral reasons plus `weather`); `scrubRequest()` puts the request back on the same day's rail with a `scrubbed` event. The slot stays used, the crew are freed. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`scrubAssignment`, `scrubRequest`, `SCRUB_REASONS`).
- **self-pilot** — `person.self_pilot`: may fly as a lone operator (R4). Seed: P-011, P-012 and P-014. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`PERSON_ROWS`) and `src/lib/rules.js` (`checkAssignment`, R4).
- **showcase** — The SF program (Showcase Flights, PRG-06): guest flights, P0, Levant, not standing, always rider-facing. The showcase run is arc D4: nine SF requests needed Jun 15–17 (eight in `SHOWCASE_RUN` plus the fixed Jun 16 showcase in `SHOWCASES`), and the desk refuses the third of a day (`R10 · desk at 3:1, ratio is 2`) while a tail and a rated crew sit free, named in the note. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`PROGRAMS`, `SHOWCASES`, `SHOWCASE_RUN`) and `src/lib/rules.js` (`validateRequest`).
- **slot** — One (date, asset, window). One assignment per slot, ever, scrubbed or not: R2 in `checkAssignment()`, `busy_sets()` in the planner, and `UNIQUE (date, asset_id, window)` on `assignments`. Defined in `sql/schema.sql` (`assignments`).
- **sortie** — See *assignment / sortie*.
- **standing program** — `program.standing`: expected to fly every weekday. `dayWarnings()` flags a standing program under target on any operating weekday even with nothing queued, a non-standing program only when it has queued demand or tails flying. Seed: PLC, PN and CQ are standing; the planner sizes their daily ask against what is already queued and plans only PLC and PN for tomorrow at the cutoff. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`dayWarnings`) and `generate_data.py` (`daily_demand`, `plan_day`).
- **surge** — Arc D2: 22 SBU requests submitted Jun 18–19 for the weekend of Jun 20–21, five of them late; `surge_ids` are exempt from the planner's repeat-deferral withdrawal draw. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`SURGE_WEEKEND`, `surge_ids`, `defer`).
- **surprise event** — Generator-only: a `STATUS_EVENTS` entry with `surprise=True` (the LV-03 and LV-05 grounding) that `asset_status_on(a, d, known_at)` hides from the planner building the previous day's plan, so the sorties already on those tails are scrubbed on the day. The flag is not exported. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`STATUS_EVENTS`, `asset_status_on`).
- **tail** — An individual aircraft: LV-01 to LV-06 (Levant), HM-01 to HM-04 (Harmattan), SR-01 and SR-02 (Sirocco), twelve in the seed. "Tail" on the screen and in rule messages, `asset` in the data. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`ASSET_ROWS`).
- **tail-day saturation** — The share of available tails that flew at all on a day, summed by week and emitted as `<airframe> tails` beside asset-window utilization; the two tell different stories in a grounding week. Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`utilizationByWeek`).
- **tell** — See *misattribution*. "The tell" is also the planner's desk-refusal note, which names the free tail and crew so the shortage is visibly neither tails nor crew. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`place`).
- **timeline** — `request.timeline`: events `{ at, day, event, reason, note, by }` in stamp order; `event` is one of `submitted`, `scheduled`, `deferred`, `rescheduled`, `reassigned`, `scrubbed`, `executed`, `withdrawn`. `eventAction()` maps each to a permission action for I4. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`event`) and `sql/schema.sql` (`request_events`).
- **today / tomorrow** — Today is the last run date, `maxDate(runsData)` in `App.jsx` and `meta.today` (2026-06-30), never the wall clock; tomorrow is today + 1 (`meta.tomorrow`, 2026-07-01), a planned day with an unscheduled queue and the board's default date. The seed spans `meta.window_start` 2026-04-02 to `meta.window_end` 2026-06-30, and board edits are stamped in the evening of today (`stampNow`). Defined in `src/lib/helpers.js` (`maxDate`) and `generate_data.py` (`END`, `TOMORROW`).
- **triage ladder** — `Open → Investigating → Corrective Action → Verified` (`TRIAGE_STEPS`). `triageFailure()` (requester only) stamps `resolved = today` on Verified and clears it on any other step unless an explicit `resolved` is passed, which is how Undo works. Triage order is unresolved first, then severity P0 to P3, then oldest first. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`TRIAGE_STEPS`, `triageFailure`) and `src/views/TriageView.jsx`.
- **utilization** — Asset utilization is used asset-windows over available asset-windows on operating days, grounded and maintenance tails out of the denominator; crew utilization is seats filled over rostered person-windows per role, with windows spent on the desk removed from the denominator. Defined in `src/lib/dispatch.js` (`assetUtilization`, `crewUtilization`, `dailyCapacity`).
- **window (AM / PM / NIGHT)** — The slot of a day a tail flies, a person is rostered or a coverer covers: `WINDOWS = ['AM', 'PM', 'NIGHT']`, `meta.windows`. Levant flies all three, Harmattan and Sirocco AM and PM. A request's `windows` is its preference list; `pruneWindows()` keeps only windows the airframe operates. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`WINDOWS`, `operatingWindows`, `pruneWindows`).
- **Windz family** — The fleet (`meta.fleet_family`): Levant, "workhorse — urban 2-pax", six tails; Harmattan, "instrumented mid-size", four; Sirocco, "bring-up airframe", two. Defined in `generate_data.py` (`AIRFRAMES`, `ASSET_ROWS`).
- **withdraw** — Taking a request out of the queue before it is on the board: `withdrawRequest()` accepts only `submitted` or `deferred` requests and sets `status: 'withdrawn'`, `deferral_reason: 'requester_withdrew'` and a `withdrawn` event. `request.withdraw` is `own` for a requester, `yes` for a coordinator, `scope` for authority; once a sortie exists the equivalent is a scrub. Defined in `src/lib/rules.js` (`withdrawRequest`) and `src/lib/auth.js` (`PERMISSIONS`).
